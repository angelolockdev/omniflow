Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about OmniFlow.

Does OmniFlow route my traffic through a proxy or VPN? +
No. OmniFlow operates inside your browser sandbox. Your video streams and creative assets load directly from Google servers at 100% of your native internet speed.
Do I need to change my Google Account country or payment profile? +
Never. OmniFlow adapts regional eligibility checks in-memory before the page router redirects, leaving your Google account settings and payment profiles untouched.
Which browsers are supported? +
Any Chromium-based browser supporting Manifest V3 (version 111+): Google Chrome, Brave, Microsoft Edge, Arc, Opera, and Vivaldi.
