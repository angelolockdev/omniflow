FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential information about OmniFlow architecture and security.

Does OmniFlow route my traffic through third-party servers?
No. OmniFlow operates purely inside your local browser runtime. All video downloads, previews, and prompt requests connect straight from Google's edge data centers to your machine.
Do I need to modify my Google account region or currency?
Never. OmniFlow handles regional parameters in-memory before the router redirects, leaving your Google Workspace, billing, and Play Store profiles untouched.
Which browsers are compatible?
Any Chromium-based browser supporting Manifest V3: Google Chrome, Brave Browser, Microsoft Edge, Arc Browser, and Opera.
